Pressure and hydraulics

How can your hand lift a car? The answer is in the oil.

‹ LessonsAll courses →

1Pressure = force on an area

📌Pressure = force ÷ area
p = F ÷ A
👠Stiletto heelsmall area = high pressure
vs
🥾Wide bootbig area = low pressure

That is why a sharp knife cuts, and a camel has wide feet on sand.

3Pressure in liquids

🌊The deeper you go in water, the higher the pressure
p = ρ · g · h

Every 10 m of water adds about 1 bar. That is why a diver’s ears hurt.

4Pascal’s principle

🔁Pressure on a trapped liquid reaches every point unchanged
F1 ÷ A1 = F2 ÷ A2
A1Small pistonsmall force, long stroke
vs
A2Big pistonbig force, short stroke

Nothing for free: the force grows but the stroke shrinks by the same ratio. The work is the same.
