Force and Newton’s laws

Just three laws, and they explain every motion around you.

‹ LessonsAll courses →

1Force = a push or a pull

💪A force starts something moving, stops it or turns it. Unit: the newton N
🍎An apple in your hand≈ 1 N
🎒A 10 kg bag98 N
🚗A car engine≈ 5000 N

2First law: inertia

🛋️Things keep doing what they are doing until a force changes it.
🚌The bus brakesyour body carries on forward
vs
🚌The bus pulls awayyour body falls back

That is the seat belt’s job: your body wants to keep the car’s speed.

3Second law: F = m·a

⚡Net force = mass × acceleration
F = m · a
🪶Small massbig acceleration
🧱Big masssmall acceleration
💪More forcemore acceleration

1 newton = the force that gives 1 kg an acceleration of 1 m/s²

4Third law: action and reaction

🔁Every force has an equal and opposite force on the other body.
🚀Rocketpushes gas down, gas pushes it up
🏊Swimmerpushes water back, water pushes them forward
🔫Riflebullet forward, rifle kicks back

5Net force

➕What causes acceleration is the net force: all the forces together.
