Torque, levers and balance

Why does a long spanner undo a stubborn nut?

‹ LessonsAll courses →

1Torque = a turning force

🔧Torque = force × arm length. Unit: N·m
τ = F · r
20 cmShort spanner200 N → 40 N·m
vs
40 cmLong spanner100 N → 40 N·m

The same torque for half the effort when the arm is twice as long.

2Where to hold it

🚪A doorpush far from the hinge
🔩A nutforce at right angles to the spanner
🚲A pedalbest push when level

A force along the spanner itself makes zero torque: it must be at right angles to the arm.

3The lever

🏗️A long arm and a small force lift a short arm and a big load.
F1 · d1 = F2 · d2
⚖️Seesawpivot in the middle
🛒Wheelbarrowload in the middle
🎣Fishing rodeffort in the middle

4The two balance conditions

1️⃣No movementsum of forces = 0
2️⃣No turningsum of torques = 0
🎯Centre of gravity: the point where all the weight seems to act. If it moves outside the base, it topples.

That is why a crane carries a counterweight, and a ladder spreads at the bottom.

5Torque and the motor

⚙️A motor delivers torque, not force. Its nameplate tells you N·m and rpm.
