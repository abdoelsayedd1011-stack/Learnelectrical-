Momentum and collisions

Why is a slow lorry more dangerous than a fast bicycle?

‹ LessonsAll courses →

1Momentum

🚚Momentum p = m × v: how hard it is to stop something.
p = m · v (kg·m/s)
8000020 t lorryat 4 m/s
vs
1000100 kg bicycleat 10 m/s

The slow lorry has 80 times the bicycle’s momentum. Very hard to stop.

2In a collision, momentum is conserved

🔒The total before the crash = the total after
m1·v1 + m2·v2 = m1·v1′ + m2·v2′

Direction matters: right is positive, left is negative.

3Elastic or sticky?

🎱Elasticthey bounce
kinetic energy conserved
vs
🧲Stickythey stick and move together
some energy is lost

Momentum is conserved in both. Only the kinetic energy differs.

4Impulse: why the airbag protects you

🛡️Impulse = force × time = change in momentum
F · t = Δp

vs
⏱️ longAirbag or stretchy ropemuch smaller force

Same when you land from a jump and bend your knees: a longer time means a smaller force.
