Weight, friction and the ramp

Friction slows you down and saves you at the same time.

‹ LessonsAll courses →

1Weight and the normal force

W = m · g
⬆️The floor pushes you up with a force equal to your weight: the normal force N.

A 50 kg crate: its weight is 490 N down, and the floor pushes 490 N up.

2Friction

🧽Friction = μ × N. It always resists motion.
f = μ · N
μsStaticbefore it moves
bigger
vs
μkKineticonce it moves
smaller

That is why the first push is the hardest, then the crate slides more easily.

3The friction coefficient μ

  • 🧊 Steel on ice
    0.1
  • 🪵 Wood on wood
    0.5
  • 🛞 Tyre on dry asphalt
    0.8
  • 🛞 Tyre on wet asphalt
    0.45

Rain roughly halves the friction. That means double the braking distance.

4Friction is your friend
