2Potential and kinetic energy

PEPotentialPE = m · g · h
stored in height
vs
KEKineticKE = ½ · m · v²
in the speed

Double the speed = four times the kinetic energy. That is why fast crashes are so destructive.

4Power = work per time

⚡Power = work ÷ time. Unit: the watt W
P = W ÷ t = F · v
1 kW1000 Wkilowatt
1 hp746 W1 hp ≈ 0.75 kW

A winch lifts 1000 kg by 10 m in 20 s: P = 1000 × 9.8 × 10 ÷ 20 = 4.9 kW
