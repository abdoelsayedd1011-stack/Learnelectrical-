Average speed: 120 km in 2 hours = 60 km/h, even if you stopped on the way.

3Acceleration = speed changing

⏩Acceleration a = change in speed every second. Unit: m/s²
a = (v − u) ÷ t
🟢Positivefoot on the gas
🔴Negativefoot on the brake
⚪Zeroconstant speed

A car going 0 to 100 km/h in 10 s: a = 27.8 ÷ 10 = 2.8 m/s²

4Three equations solve any problem

1️⃣Speed after a timev = u + a·t
2️⃣Distance after a timed = u·t + ½·a·t²
3️⃣Without timev² = u² + 2·a·d

u = starting speed, v = final speed.

5Braking distance

🛑From the third equation: stopping distance = v² ÷ 2a

Double the speed = four times the braking distance. That is why speed kills.
