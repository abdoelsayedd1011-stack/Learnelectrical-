Free fall and projectiles

Everything falls with the same acceleration. Even when you throw it sideways.

1Gravity = a constant acceleration

🌍A falling object gains 9.8 m/s of speed every second
g = 9.8 m/s²

⏱️After 2 s19.6 m/s · 19.6 m

3Falling from a height

h = ½ · g · t²
t = √(2h ÷ g)

Something dropped from the 10th floor (30 m): t = √(60 ÷ 9.8) ≈ 2.5 s, and it lands at 24 m/s, about 88 km/h.

A small spanner falling from scaffolding can injure badly. The helmet is not decoration.

4A projectile = two motions at once

🏀Sideways: constant speed. Up and down: gravity. Each minds its own business.
