How to use it

Quick steps

  1. Open files: pick one file or more. With several files, their pages are placed one after another; that is the merge.
  2. Arrange: drag pages or use the arrows, and rotate, delete or duplicate any page with the buttons under it.
  3. Type or sign: tap a page to open it large. Add text (Arabic or English), a hand-drawn signature, an image (a stamp, say), a white box to cover old text, or a highlight. Drag the item into place and resize it from the blue dot.
  4. Split: tap the scissors ✂ between any two pages to cut the file there, or choose “One file per page”.
  5. Save: download the whole file, only the selected pages, the parts you cut, or turn the pages into images.

Note: the tool adds text and images on top of the page; it does not change the text already inside the file. To change a word, cover it with a white box and type over it, or use text with a white background.
