Temperature 1: thermocouples

The thermocouple is the simplest temperature sensor there is: two wires of different metals welded together at one end. It handles up to 1700 °C, it's cheap and it's fast. But it has a "secret" that, if you don't understand it, will have you reading wrong for the rest of your career: the cold junction. In this lesson you will understand it and try it yourself on a bench with real tables.

8Common faults

  • Reads room temperaturewires touching somewhere cold
  • Falls as the furnace heatsreversed polarity
  • Steady 20–40° errorwrong type, copper wire, or CJC off
  • OPENbroken: measure ohms (good = a few Ω, broken = OL)
  • Jumpinggrounded type or near power cables
  • K drifts up at 800–1000 °Cgreen rot: replace or use N

Before pulling a sensor from a steam-line thermowell: make sure the well isn’t holed.
