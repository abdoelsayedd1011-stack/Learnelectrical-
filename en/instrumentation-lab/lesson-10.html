1The loop check

🔁Before start-up, every loop is checked from sensor to HMI.

  1. Visual: tag and range
  2. Continuity + megger (devices disconnected)
  3. Inject 0 · 50 · 100 %
  4. Alarms and interlocks
  5. Valve 0 → 100 % + feedback
  6. Record and sign

4How to think about any fault

  1. Compare with an independent reading
  2. Measure mA: split the loop
  3. Read the number
  4. What changed?
  5. Fix and record the cause
  • 0 mAOpen loop
  • > 21 mAShort or fail
  • Stuck, not movingIsolation valve · blocked line · weak supply
  • JumpingNoise
  • mA right, PLC wrongCard or scaling
