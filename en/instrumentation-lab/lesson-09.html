Pressure, level and flow

These three variables are the daily work of any plant handling liquids: water, steam, oil, milk, chemicals. You will learn the types of pressure transmitter, how a simple pressure reading at the bottom of a tank tells you the level (and when it lies to you), and how to choose the right flowmeter without wasting the plant's money.
