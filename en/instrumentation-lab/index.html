Instrumentation course

Instrumentation is the plant's "senses": the sensors that measure temperature, pressure, weight, speed and position, and send the reading to the PLC so it can control the process. In this course you will really understand the 4–20 mA signal, wire PNP and NPN sensors, and get hands-on with encoders, thermocouples, PT100s and load cells: calibrate them and find their faults like a field technician.

Before you start: this course assumes you understand voltage, current and resistance and can use a multimeter. If not yet, start with the Electrical basics course → and come back here.

How to study here

  1. Read the explanation: one idea per section, in plain words
  2. Try it on the bench: turn the encoder, heat the thermocouple, put weights on the load cell
  3. Create the faults yourself: cut a wire, reverse the polarity, and see what the reading does
  4. Answer the questions to mark the lesson as done
