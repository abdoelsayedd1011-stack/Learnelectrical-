Temperature 2: PT100, RTD and NTC

The PT100 is the king of accuracy in plants: a platinum resistance that rises with temperature in a precise, well-known way. More accurate and stable than a thermocouple up to about 500 °C. But it has an enemy called lead-wire resistance. Here you will see that error in numbers, understand why there are 2, 3 and 4 wires, and measure the sensor with a multimeter.
