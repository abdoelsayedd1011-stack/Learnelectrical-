Refund policy

A course is digital content that opens immediately and stays open for 1 year, so here are the rules, clearly, up front.

Within 7 days: your money back in full

If you ask for a refund within 7 days of payment, we refund the full amount, as long as you've used only a small part of the paid content (no more than two lessons or two exercises of the paid content).

How to ask

Message us on WhatsApp or by email with your account email and the payment date.

WhatsApp Email

Contact options will be added here soon.

How the money comes back

The same way you paid (InstaPay, Vodafone Cash or card), within about 14 days of approving the request. Once refunded, the course is closed.

After 7 days

No refund, but the course stays open until the last day of its year.

No automatic renewal

Payment is manual, so nobody can charge you without you paying yourself. When the year ends, the course closes until you renew it.

Account sharing

An account is for one person. If it's shared with someone else, we may close it without a refund.
