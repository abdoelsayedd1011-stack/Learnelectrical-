1Starting a new servo for the first time

Technicians who commission servos properly follow a fixed order, so any mistake shows up early and with the least damage. Put the steps in order:

Order the commissioning steps

Safety: the first move is always at low speed, with the E-stop in your hand, the axis in the middle, and people aware that the machine is going to move. Vertical axes: support the load before any brake test.

2The next step: the simulators

Finished the explanation? Now practise on devices with their keypad, display and parameters, each one like the real thing:
  • Delta ASDA-B2: PT, PR, S and T modes and pulses from a PLC.
  • Mitsubishi MR-JE: the keypad, the gear and moving machines.
  • Yaskawa Sigma-7: Fn, JOG and the gear.
  • Allen-Bradley Kinetix 300: indexing and MotionView.

Or see the whole servo course page. Remember that parameter numbers and alarms differ from brand to brand, and that is exactly what you will get used to there.

★Final exam

15 questions from the whole course. You need at least 12 correct. If you pass, and you have also finished all the exercises in the lessons, the certificate appears on the course page.

The numbers and values in this lesson (voltages, frequencies, gains, limits) are approximate training values based on general field experience, and they differ from brand to brand and model to model: your drive's manual is the reference before any setup on a real machine. Alarm and parameter names also differ from one manufacturer to another. Your progress is saved on this device.
