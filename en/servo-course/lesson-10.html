2Pulses: frequency and output types

Speed (rpm) = frequency (Hz) ÷ pulses per rev × 60
  • Open collector (transistor): the normal high-speed PLC output. Its limit is roughly tens to hundreds of kHz, and the cable must be short (a few metres).
  • Line driver (differential): each signal on two inverted wires; it tolerates noise, longer distances and frequencies in the MHz range.
  • The drive returns signals to the PLC such as In-position (arrived), Alarm and Ready.

3Industrial networks

On modern machines the drive is usually connected with a network cable. Every cycle (every 1 ms, for example) the PLC sends the target position for each axis, and the drive replies with the actual position, the torque and any alarm.

  • EtherCAT: one frame passes through all the drives, and each one reads and writes its part on the fly. Very fast and widespread in motion control.
  • PROFINET (with PROFIdrive / IRT): widespread with Siemens.
  • EtherNet/IP (CIP Motion): widespread with Allen-Bradley.
  • Other manufacturer-specific networks such as CC-Link IE, MECHATROLINK and others.
  • Modbus RS-485 / TCP: relatively slow, good for monitoring and writing parameters, not for fast synchronised motion.
