Wiring the servo

We will wire a servo drive from start to finish: power, motor, encoder, brake, braking resistor, the PLC and the safety circuit. And you will see for yourself why swapping U and W on a servo is nothing like on an induction motor.

2The motor: U V W and PE — the order is mandatory

⚠️Swap two phases and the motor shakes or runs away. Reverse it with a parameter.

  • ✅ U→U · V→V · W→W · PEA ready-made cable is best
  • ❌ Output contactorNo capacitors either

  • 🔗 Encoder CN2360° shield · away from power · high-flex in a chain
