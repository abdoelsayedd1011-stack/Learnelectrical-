Inside the drive

The drive has two parts: a power circuit exactly like a VFD, and a control section with three nested loops that run thousands of times per second. In this lesson you will see both in an interactive diagram.

‹ LessonsAll courses →ع

1The power circuit: exactly like a VFD

  1. Rectifier: AC → DC
  2. DC bus: ≈ 310 V (220V) · 540 V (400V)
  3. IGBTs → U V W
  4. Braking resistor burns returned energy
Wait for the CHARGE lamp to go out and measure P+ ↔ N before touching.

2The control: 3 nested loops

🪆Each loop commands the one inside it, and the inner one is faster.

  • 📍 Position (outer)Asks for speed · tens of Hz
  • ⏩ Speed (middle)Asks for torque · hundreds of Hz
  • ⚡ Current (inner)Outputs PWM · several kHz

Like driving: 50 m left ⇒ go 40 ⇒ press the pedal.
