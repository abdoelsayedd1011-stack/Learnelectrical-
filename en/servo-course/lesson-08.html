Tuning and gain setting

The same motor and the same drive can be smooth and fast or humming and shaking, and the whole difference is in the gain setting. You will try auto-tune, then tune by hand and watch the curve change in front of you.

1What does tuning mean?

A new drive from the factory does not know what the motor is carrying: a light belt or a 200 kg table. Tuning means telling the drive "this is the load" and setting how fast the loops react, so the motion is fast and free of vibration.

The inertia ratio (JL/JM): the inertia of the rotating load (referred to the motor shaft) ÷ the inertia of the motor itself. A ratio of 10 means the load is 10 times "heavier" than the rotor. The drive uses it to turn "I want this acceleration" into the right torque. If the number in the parameter is wrong, all the gains will be wrong. And the bigger the ratio (above 10 to 30 depending on the motor), the harder the control, and then a higher-inertia motor or a gearbox solves it.

2The gains and the symptoms

ParameterIf too lowIf too high
Kv speed gain (Hz)Slow, affected by the loadHumming and whistling, fast vibration, the motor heats up
Ti speed integral time(small Ti = strong integral) slow oscillation(large Ti) takes time to close the last error
Kp position gain (rad/s)Large following error and slow stoppingOvershoot and ringing when stopping
Inertia ratio(lower than actual) overshoot and loose motion(much higher) like a high Kv: humming
  • Overshoot: the axis passes the target and comes back.
  • Settling time: the time from the end of the command until the axis is inside the in-position window and settled.
  • Sluggish: arrives late and without overshoot.

3Try it: auto-tune, then by hand

Gain setting on a real load

  1. Make sure the mechanics are sound (no binding and no play in the coupling).
  2. Run auto-tune (or let the drive measure the inertia ratio) on a move close to the real one.
  3. Raise Kv gradually until you hear humming, then lower it by 20 to 30%.
  4. Raise Kp until the stop is fast without overshoot.
  5. Adjust Ti if a small error remains. And if there is resonance at a certain frequency, use a notch filter.
Auto-tune differs between modern drives: there is online (learns while running) and offline (a test move), and there is a "rigidity level" that you choose. The names and steps are in each brand's manual.
