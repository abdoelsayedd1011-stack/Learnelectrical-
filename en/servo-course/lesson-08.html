Tuning and gain setting

The same motor and the same drive can be smooth and fast or humming and shaking, and the whole difference is in the gain setting. You will try auto-tune, then tune by hand and watch the curve change in front of you.

‹ LessonsAll courses →ع

1What does tuning mean?

🎚️Tell the drive "this is the load" so motion is fast and vibration-free.

Inertia ratio JL/JM: how many times heavier the load is than the rotor. Wrong value = every gain wrong. Above 10–30 ⇒ gearbox or higher-inertia motor.

2The gains and the symptoms

  • 🔊 Buzzing, whiningKv high (or inertia set too high)
  • 🐢 Slow, load-sensitiveKv low
  • 〰️ Slow oscillationTi too small
  • ↩️ Overshoot, ringingKp high (or inertia set too low)
  • 📏 Big following errorKp low
OvershootPasses the target and returns
SettlingUntil in-position
SluggishLate, no overshoot
