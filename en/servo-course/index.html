Servo motor and drive

A servo is what makes a machine go to the exact position at the exact speed: packaging, CNC, cut-to-length, robots. In this course you will understand the motor, the encoder and the drive from the inside, wire them, calculate the electronic gear, set the gains, run a homing sequence, drive the axis from a PLC, and clear its alarms. All of this before you open the device simulators.

Before you start: it helps to understand electrical basics and the 3-phase motor, and knowing the VFD makes things easier. If not yet, start with the electrical basics course and have a look at VFDs.
When you finish: practise on the servo simulators (Delta, Mitsubishi, Yaskawa and Allen-Bradley).

How to study here

  1. Read the explanation: one idea per part, in plain words
  2. Try it yourself: wire the drive, set the gains, run a homing
  3. Cause the faults: swap U and W, brake too fast, break the limit switch wire
  4. Answer the questions so the lesson is marked complete
