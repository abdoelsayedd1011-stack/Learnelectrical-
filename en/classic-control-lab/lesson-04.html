Lesson 4: Protection

The circuit you built in lesson 3 works, but if there's a short circuit or the motor is overloaded, nothing protects it. Here you'll learn the fuse and the MCB (for short circuits), the thermal overload relay (for overload and phase loss), and the MPCB that combines them, and wire them into a complete DOL circuit with a fault lamp. The overload here heats up and trips in real time, so try loading the motor and wait.
