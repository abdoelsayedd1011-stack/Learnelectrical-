Lesson 7: Pressure switches, level switches and sensors

So far the circuit has been operated by a person (a button or a selector) or by the machine (a limit switch). In this lesson the process itself is in control: the pressure in the receiver, the level in the tank, or an object passing in front of a sensor. You'll learn the pressure switch, the float, PNP/NPN proximity sensors and the photocell, and wire 6 real circuits: a pump with a float, a compressor, a conveyor with a photocell, and dry-run protection.

Symbols according to IEC 60617 and NEMA ICS 19. Sensor wire colours according to IEC 60947-5-2: BN brown +24V, BU blue 0V, BK black the output (and WH white the second output on 4-wire sensors). Contact numbers 13-14 NO and 21-22 NC. The pressures and times in the simulation are chosen so you can see the cycle quickly; in reality they are set to suit the machine.
