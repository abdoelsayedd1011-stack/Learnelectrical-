How to study here

  1. Definition: what it is and where it's used
  2. Look and symbol: the real device and its IEC and NEMA symbols
  3. Try it: press, turn and move it, and watch the contacts
  4. Wire it: terminal → terminal, then switch on the power
  5. Questions: quick ones to make sure you've understood
