Classic Control Course

Interactive control course: buttons, contactor, overload and motor, then the starter circuits in order, then the timer and its circuits such as star-delta. Every circuit shows power, control and the panel, and runs when you press start.

The course runs in order: first the devices (buttons, contactor, overload, motor), then the basic circuits one by one, then the timer and its circuits.
