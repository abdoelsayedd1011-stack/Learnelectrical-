Lesson 9: Advanced topics: star/delta, braking, the AC compressor, and the soft starter

The last lesson in the course, with the four topics you'll meet in large panels: star/delta for the starting current, braking so the motor stops on time, the central AC compressor circuit on 24V with pressure switches and a delay, and the soft starter, which raises the voltage gently. Each topic: definition → look and symbol → simulation → hands-on wiring → questions, and every exercise has its own circuit diagram.

Symbols according to IEC 60617 and NEMA ICS 19, and the Y-Δ timer terminal numbers (17-18 / 17-28) and soft starter terminals (13-14 · 23-24 · 95-96) according to common catalogues. Timer and ramp times in the simulation are short so you can see the result quickly: in reality the compressor's anti-short-cycle timer is 3 minutes, and the soft starter ramp is 5–30 seconds.
