Lesson 8: Sequencing, jogging and reversing exercises (practicals 5–13)

In this lesson we continue the practical workbook from exercise 5 to exercise 13. They all build on what you've learned: the contactor and self-holding (lesson 3) and the overload (lesson 4). What's new here is the auxiliary contact as a condition between two motors, the double button for jogging and direct reversing, and the electrical and mechanical interlock, without which a reversing circuit makes a short circuit. Every exercise: a power circuit + a control circuit + pilot lamps, and its own circuit diagram.

Symbols according to IEC 60617 and NEMA ICS 19. Auxiliary contact numbers according to IEC 60947-5-1: 13-14, 23-24 and 33-34 NO, and 21-22 NC (the first digit counts the contact and the second is its function: 3-4 NO, 1-2 NC). The hoist and cart travel times in the simulation are chosen so you can see the result quickly.
