Lesson 3: The contactor

The contactor is the big relay that runs the motor. You'll learn its parts and terminal numbers, the difference between the power circuit and the control circuit, and then wire your first real circuit: a 3-phase motor started and stopped from one place + pilot lamps (practical exercise no. 1), then from two places, from 3 places, and two motors.

Symbols according to IEC 60617 and NEMA ICS 19. Contactor terminals according to IEC 60947-4-1: main poles 1/L1-2/T1, 3/L2-4/T2, 5/L3-6/T3, coil A1-A2, and auxiliary contacts 13-14 (NO) and 21-22 (NC). Utilisation categories AC-1 (resistive loads) and AC-3 (squirrel-cage motors) according to IEC 60947-4-1. The thermal overload and protection are in the next lesson.
