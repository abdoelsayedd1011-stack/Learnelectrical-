Lesson 6: The 3-phase induction motor

Every circuit so far has run a “motor”, and now we'll open it up: what's inside and how it turns, how to read the nameplate and get the current, cable size and overload setting from it, how to open the terminal box and fit the star or delta links correctly (and what happens if you get it wrong), and how to reverse the direction. The simulation here calculates the voltage on each winding and the current, and the overload trips if you wire it wrong.

Symbols according to IEC 60617 and NEMA ICS 19. Motor terminals according to IEC 60034-8: U1 V1 W1 are the winding starts and U2 V2 W2 their ends, and the top row in the terminal box is W2 U2 V2 so the delta links are vertical. The power and current figures are approximate catalogue values for IE3 four-pole motors at 400 V, and the cable size table is a rule of thumb (PVC copper in conduit, 30 °C); for real installations check the motor data sheet and your local code tables.
