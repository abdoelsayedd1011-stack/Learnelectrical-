3OEE: the number a manager understands

OEE = A × P × Q
  • Availability
    87.5 %
  • Performance
    83.3 %
  • Quality
    96.7 %
  • OEE
    70.5 %

Signals: Running + part counter + reject counter.

85% is world class; most plants start at 40–60%. What matters is where the minutes go: a Pareto of stop reasons.

4Designing a dashboard that does not confuse
