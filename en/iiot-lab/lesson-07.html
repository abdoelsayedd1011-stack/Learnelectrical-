4DeviceNet: the key difference

🔋CAN + CIP, and the cable carries 24 V with the signal.

  • ⚪ White / 🔵 blueCAN_H / CAN_L
  • 🔴 Red / ⚫ blackV+ / V−
  • MAC ID0–63 · 125/250/500 kbit/s
  • NS steady greenConnected to a master
  • NS flashing greenOnline, no master
  • NS flashing redTimeout
  • NS steady redDuplicate MAC ID or bus off

Below 11 V at the far end ⇒ strange faults · add a supply mid-line.

5Reasoning through a CAN fault

  1. 60 Ω between H and L
  2. Idle: H ≈ L ≈ 2.5 V
  3. Bus off: baud · cable · duplicate ID
  4. Drops with a machine: noise
  5. Far devices only: voltage drop
