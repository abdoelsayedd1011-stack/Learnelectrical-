Names: lowercase, digits and hyphens only, like vfd-line3. Replaced a device? Give it the same name.

3Topology and switches

➖LineOne break ⇒ everything after is lost
✳️StarManaged switch · safer
⭕MRP ringSurvives one break · 200 ms

Industrial switches that support PROFINET. LLDP reveals a cable in the wrong port.

4LEDs and diagnostics

  • 🔴 BF steadyNo link or no controller
  • 🔴 BF flashingPresent but no data: name · IP · not in plant
  • 🔴 SFMissing module · broken wire
  • 🟡 MTCable dropping frames

TIA: diagnostics buffer · free: PRONETA.

5Common PROFINET faults

  • New device not foundName not set or has a capital
  • BF flashing after a swapOld name · reset, then set the name
  • Drops every few minutesDuplicate IP · cable near a VFD
  • All devices past a point goneLine with a cut cable
  • IRT won't runPlain switch · topology mismatch
