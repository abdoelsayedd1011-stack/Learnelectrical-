PROFIBUS DP

The purple cable found in almost every Siemens panel. Nearly all of its faults are physical: termination, addressing, connectors. Learn to read BF and SF and know exactly where to look.

  1. Address 0–125 on the device

Switches say 5, project says 4 ⇒ BF, no data. Some devices read the switches only at power-up.

4LEDs and diagnostics

  • 🔴 BF steadyNo communication: cable · termination · baud
  • 🔴 BF flashingAt least one slave missing
  • 🔴 SFConfiguration or slave diagnostic
  • 🔴 DIA on a slaveRead the diagnostic from the master

TIA: accessible devices · on the cable: ProfiTrace.

5Reasoning through a PROFIBUS fault

  • Whole network at onceEnd device off · connector unplugged
  • One slaveIts address · power · a break before it?
  • Intermittent faultsLower the baud: gone ⇒ physical problem
  • After a replacementSame address · power-cycle · matching GSD
