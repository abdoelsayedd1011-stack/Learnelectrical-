2Topics and wildcards

factory/line3/oven/temperature
  • +One level · factory/+/oven/temperature
  • #Everything below · factory/line3/#
  • $SYS/Broker statistics

Wildcards in subscriptions only. No spaces, no capitals, no leading /.

3QoS, Retain and Last Will

QoS 0At most onceTemperature every second
QoS 1At least onceAlarms, counters · most common
QoS 2Exactly onceCommands · heaviest
  • RetainLast value to any new subscriber at once
  • Last Willgateway/status = offline on a drop
  • Keep aliveMissing 1.5 × N ⇒ the will is published
  • Persistent sessionKeeps missed QoS 1/2 messages
