1How does publish and subscribe work?

In MQTT two devices never connect directly. The publisher sends a message under an address called a topic to the broker, and the broker hands it to every subscriber of that topic.

PublishertopicBrokertopicSubscriber

Because nobody knows anybody else, you can add a dashboard or a phone without touching the PLC.

Port 1883 is unencrypted and 8883 uses TLS. Well-known brokers are Mosquitto, EMQX, HiveMQ and the AWS and Azure services. The payload is any bytes: a number, text or JSON. And the connection goes out from the plant to the broker, so no ports are opened in the plant.

2How are topics written?

A topic is a path of levels separated by slashes:

factory/line3/oven/temperature

When subscribing you can use wildcards. + matches one level, like factory/+/oven/temperature. # matches everything after it, like factory/line3/#. And topics starting with $SYS/ are the broker's own statistics.

Wildcards are for subscribing only. And no spaces, no capitals and no leading /.

3What are QoS, Retain and Last Will?

QoS sets the delivery guarantee. QoS 0 is at most once, good for a temperature sent every second. QoS 1 is at least once, good for alarms and counters, and the most common. QoS 2 is exactly once, good for commands, and the heaviest.

Retain makes the broker keep the last value and give it to any new subscriber at once. Last Will is a message the broker publishes when the client drops, like gateway/status = offline.

Keep alive is the silence limit: if the client is absent for 1.5 × N, the will is published. And a persistent session keeps the QoS 1 and 2 messages the client missed.

4What is Sparkplug B for?

MQTT imposes no shape on the topic or the payload. Sparkplug B sets one standard for both:

spBv1.0/group/type/node/device

NBIRTH is a birth message carrying all the metrics. DDATA is sent on change only. NDEATH is the will. And NCMD is a command addressed to the node.

System supports it (Ignition)? Sparkplug B. Otherwise fixed, documented JSON: {"v":182.5,"u":"C","t":…}

5How do I try it in practice?

To subscribe to all plant topics and print them:

mosquitto_sub -t "factory/#" -v

To publish a value with Retain:

mosquitto_pub -t factory/line3/oven/temperature -m 182.5 -r

From the PLC: with the LMQTT library or MQTT blocks, or through the gateway, which is the most common. Security: a user per gateway with TLS and ACLs. And volume: send on change, not every 100 ms.
