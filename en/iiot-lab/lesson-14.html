▶ The lesson in 5 minutes

Illustrated explanation, the simulator task solved, and the quiz answered. Made for the phone.

1Where do I start diagnosing?

Don't guess. Go up layer by layer from the bottom, with a question and a tool for each layer.

1 Physical: is there a signal on the wire? Tool: LEDs and an ohmmeter. 2 Data link: does the switch see it? Tool: the MAC table and LLDP. 3 Network: does the IP answer? Tool: ping and arp.

4 Transport: is the port open? Tool: telnet ip 502. And 7 Application: is the answer correct? Tool: a protocol client and Wireshark.

Half the faults are in layer 1: check the LEDs, then the connectors, then the termination, then the voltage.

2What are the diagnostic tools?

Wireshark captures frames from a SPAN port, and many retransmissions there mean a physical problem. PRONETA draws the PROFINET network map. And ProfiTrace measures PROFIBUS signal quality.

An oscilloscope shows reflections, and a reflection means a termination problem. MQTT Explorer and UaExpert check MQTT and OPC UA. And before all of that read the device log, which states the cause in words.

3Which ten cases will you meet?

RTU sometimes reads zeros: noise, termination or too much length. Exception 02: the address is off by one. PROFIBUS drops when a motor starts: the cable runs next to power cables, or a shield problem. PROFINET fails after a replacement: the device name.

EtherNet/IP drops at random: IGMP snooping or RPI. CAN bus-off: one node at a different baud, or the resistance is not 60 Ω. Ping works but the protocol doesn't: a firewall or the PUT/GET setting.

BadCertificateUntrusted: move the certificate to Trusted. MQTT connects and drops: a duplicate client ID or keep-alive. And a frozen value on the dashboard: a stopped gateway plus a retained message.

4The full line project

In front of you is a filling line with 3 hidden faults in different layers. The line is made of an S7-1200 with PROFINET I/O, a conveyor on Modbus RTU, a pressure sensor on IO-Link, a servo on CANopen, and a gateway that turns OPC UA into MQTT.

The task: find each fault with the right tool, fix it, then confirm on the dashboard.

On the simulator: in the Faults tab, fix all three faults until every indicator is green. Open the simulator →
