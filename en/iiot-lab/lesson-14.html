1The troubleshooting method: bottom up

🪜Don't guess: layer by layer, one question and one tool each.

  • 1 PhysicalSignal on the wire? · LEDs · ohmmeter
  • 2 LinkDoes the switch see it? · MAC table · LLDP
  • 3 NetworkDoes IP reach? · ping · arp
  • 4 TransportPort open? · telnet ip 502
