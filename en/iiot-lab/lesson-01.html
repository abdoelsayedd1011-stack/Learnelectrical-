1The plant talks, but in different languages

📡IIoT carries shop-floor data to the cloud, so you see it on your phone before a failure.

  • ⬆️ TopOPC UA · MQTT: lots of data, not urgent
  • ↔️ MiddlePROFINET · EtherNet/IP · Modbus TCP
  • ⬇️ BottomPROFIBUS · CAN · Modbus RTU · IO-Link: small, fast, deterministic
Going down: smaller, faster, more deterministic. Going up: bigger and richer in meaning.
