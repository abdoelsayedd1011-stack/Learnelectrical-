Predictive Maintenance

The purpose of every network so far: knowing a bearing will fail before it does. Here you learn what to measure, and how to turn the numbers into a planned maintenance decision.

▶ The lesson in 5 minutes

Illustrated explanation, the simulator task solved, and the quiz answered. Made for the phone.

1Three kinds of maintenance

🚒ReactiveAfter the failureSudden stops and damage
📅PreventiveEvery N hoursReplaces good parts
📈PredictiveWhen data shows declinePlanned stops

Predictive turns preventive from "by calendar" into "by condition".

2What do you measure?

📳Vibration is the key indicator: RMS in mm/s against ISO 10816.

  • Good
    < 2.8
  • Acceptable
    < 7.1
  • Unacceptable
    > 11
  • FFT 1×Unbalance
  • FFT 2×Misalignment
  • Bearing frequenciesBearing damage
🌡️TemperatureBearings and panels
⚡CurrentFree from the VFD
🔢CountersLife by operations
🧪OthersFilter ΔP · oil · ultrasound

3From measurement to decision

  1. Two-week baseline
  2. Warn +2σ · alarm +3σ
  3. Slope ⇒ RUL
  4. Same operating state
  5. CMMS work order
Machine learning comes after a year of clean data. Until then limits and trends solve 80%.
