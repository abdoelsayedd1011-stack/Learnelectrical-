Braking and stopping

Starting is easy; stopping is what causes trouble. When you stop a heavy load fast, the motor turns into a generator and sends the energy back into the drive. Try it yourself: you will cause an OV fault, then fix it.

1Ways to stop

MethodWhat happensWhen
Ramp to stopThe drive lowers the frequency over the decel timeThe most common: conveyors, pumps
Coast to stopThe drive cuts its output and the load stops on frictionHeavy fans, or when the time does not matter
DC injectionThe drive puts DC on the windings, which brakes and holdsAt the end of a stop so the shaft does not creep (the energy is burnt in the motor)
Braking resistorThe chopper burns the returned energy in a resistorFast stops of heavy loads, hoists, centrifuges
Regen / AFESends the energy back to the supplyLarge loads that brake a lot (lifts, big hoists)

2Why OV?

When the drive lowers the frequency faster than the load would stop on its own, the load turns the motor faster than the new synchronous speed ⇒ the motor works as a generator and charges the capacitors. The rectifier cannot send energy back to the supply, so the voltage rises to the OV limit (on a 400 V drive, roughly 800 V DC) and the drive trips.

Many drives have OV stall prevention / a bus regulator: when the voltage rises, it lengthens the decel by itself. That prevents the fault, but the stop takes longer than you set. If you use a braking resistor, you usually have to turn this feature off.

4The right braking resistor

  • The resistance (Ω) must not be below the minimum value in the manual, or the chopper burns.
  • The power (W) must suit the energy and the braking duty cycle: a load that stops every minute is not the same as one that stops once an hour.
  • The resistor gets very hot: outside or on top of the panel, away from cables.
  • Use the resistor's thermal switch on a drive input or in the contactor circuit.
  • Enable the chopper in the parameters (on many brands it is off from the factory).
From the field: a centrifuge kept tripping on OV. The technician set a 120-second decel «to be done with it». Production complained. The fix was a braking resistor with the value from the manual, and the decel came down to 20 seconds with no faults.
