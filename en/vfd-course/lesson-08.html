Braking and stopping

Starting is easy; stopping is what causes trouble. When you stop a heavy load fast, the motor turns into a generator and sends the energy back into the drive. Try it yourself: you will cause an OV fault, then fix it.

‹ LessonsAll courses →ع

1Ways to stop

  • Ramp to stopLowers frequency over decel · conveyors, pumps
  • Coast to stopCuts the output · heavy fans
  • DC injectionHolds at the end of the stop
  • Braking resistorBurns returned energy · hoists, centrifuges
  • Regen / AFEReturns energy to the mains · lifts

2Why OV?

  1. Decel faster than the load
  2. Motor becomes a generator
  3. DC bus rises
  4. ≈ 800 V DC ⇒ OV

The bus regulator stretches decel by itself. Using a resistor? Turn it off.
