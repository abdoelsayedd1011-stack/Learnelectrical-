PWM and V/f

An IGBT is a switch: either on or off. How can a switch alone make a smooth sine wave at any frequency? The answer: PWM. And why, when we lower the frequency, do we have to lower the voltage with it? The answer: V/f. Both are in this simulator.

3Why constant V/f?

🧲Flux = V ÷ f. Keep it constant ⇒ full torque at any speed.

V/f = 400 V ÷ 50 Hz = 8 V/Hz
  • High V · low fSaturation and heat
  • Low V · high fWeak torque
  • Below 10 HzBoost compensates
  • Above 50 HzField weakening: more speed, less torque

Pumps and fans: a quadratic V/f curve saves more.
