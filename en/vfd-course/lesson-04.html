Power wiring

The most expensive VFD mistake happens in the first minute: the supply on the motor terminals. In this lesson you learn where every power terminal goes, wire a complete board yourself, and learn the rules that keep the drive from burning.

The rule: supply on the input, motor on the output, and nothing else goes between them and the drive unless the manual says so.
A single-phase drive: its input is L N 230 V and its output is 3 phase 230 V. So the motor must be 230/400 V and connected in delta. If it is connected in star it runs below its rated voltage with weak torque.

3Rules that protect the drive

  • No contactor opens on the output while the drive is running. If you need a contactor (for a bypass, for example), it switches only with the drive stopped.
  • No power factor capacitors and no ordinary surge arresters on the drive output.
  • Shielded motor cable (or run in metal conduit), with the shield earthed 360° in the cable gland at both ends.
  • Control cables kept away from motor cables (about 30 cm), and if they cross, at a right angle.
  • Protection before the drive (fuses or a breaker) of the type and size in the manual, not by guesswork.
  • The main contactor before the drive is for isolation and emergencies, not for starting and stopping the motor again and again.
