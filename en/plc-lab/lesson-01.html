What is a PLC?

A PLC (Programmable Logic Controller) is a small, rugged computer for factories: it handles heat, dust and electrical noise, reads buttons and sensors, and runs motors and valves according to a program you write. Here you will see why factories left relay circuits behind, and how a PLC "thinks" in a cycle called the scan.
