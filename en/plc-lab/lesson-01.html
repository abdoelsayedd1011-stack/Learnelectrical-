What is a PLC?

A PLC (Programmable Logic Controller) is a small, rugged computer for factories: it handles heat, dust and electrical noise, reads buttons and sensors, and runs motors and valves according to a program you write. Here you will see why factories left relay circuits behind, and how a PLC "thinks" in a cycle called the scan.

1Why did the PLC replace relays?

In an old relay panel the program is the wiring itself: any change to how the machine works means rewiring. In a PLC the program is written on a laptop, so a change takes minutes.

A machine that needed 30 relays and 8 timers now has them all as lines in the program.

The PLC doesn't replace contactors or overloads. And the emergency stop must cut by wiring or a safety relay, not by the program.

2How does a PLC work?

The PLC runs in a loop called the scan cycle, three steps that repeat: it reads all inputs, runs the program, then writes the outputs. Then it starts again. The whole cycle takes 1 to 20 ms.

If a signal is shorter than one scan, the PLC may never see it.

3What is a PLC made of?

There are two shapes. A compact PLC is all in one box. A modular PLC is a rack where you add the cards you need. Tap each card to see what it does:

PLC rack

There are four card types. DI is a digital input for buttons and sensors (0 or 24V DC). DO is a digital output for contactors and lamps, relay or transistor type. AI is an analog input for transmitters (4–20mA or 0–10V). And AO is an analog output for VFD speed or a valve opening.

4Which brands are well known?

Siemens: S7-1200 and S7-1500 with TIA Portal. Allen-Bradley: Micro800 and CompactLogix with Studio 5000. Schneider: M221 and M241 with Machine Expert.

Mitsubishi: FX5U with GX Works. Delta: DVP and AS with ISPSoft. And Omron: CP1E and NX1P with Sysmac.

All of them support Ladder (plus FBD, ST and SFC). We focus on Ladder.

5Faults you meet on site

All outputs off: the PLC is in STOP, check the RUN/STOP switch. ERROR or SF lit: a card is missing or loose, or there is a power problem.

All inputs went off at once: the 24V dropped because of a shorted sensor. And the program is lost: the memory battery died, so always keep a backup.
