Analog signals in the PLC

A digital sensor says "there is" or "there isn't". But a level transmitter says "the tank holds 2.35 metres". That number reaches the PLC as a 4–20mA current, the card converts it to a raw number, and you must convert that to the real value before you use it. This is called scaling.

The 0–27648 range is used by Siemens S7-1200 / S7-1500 cards for a 4–20mA signal. Other brands use 0–32000, 0–4000 or 0–65535, or give you the value already in engineering units: the card's datasheet is the reference. The simulator here is a generic trainer with IEC / Siemens style addresses: the exact addresses and instructions differ from one brand to another, and the manual of your PLC is the reference.
