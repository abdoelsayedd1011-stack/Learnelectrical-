Counters and comparison

Many machines are all about "counting": 6 cans per box, 100 turns per reel, the number of cars in a car park. In this lesson you will use the CTU and CTD counters and compare numbers with compare instructions, on machines that move in front of you.

In Allen-Bradley the CTU and CTD instructions can both work on the same counter C5:0, and TIA Portal has a single CTUD block. In the simulator, CTU and CTD on the same address work on the same value, and the counter does not go below zero. The simulator here is a generic trainer with IEC / Siemens style addresses: the exact addresses and instructions differ from one brand to another, and the manual of your PLC is the reference.
