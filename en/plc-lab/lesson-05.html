Timers

In classic control every timer was a part mounted in the panel. In a PLC you have hundreds of timers inside the program, and you change their time by typing a number. Here you will understand the three types TON, TOF and TP on a live chart, and write a complete star-delta program.

Timer names and shapes differ: in TIA Portal an IEC_Timer block with IN, PT, Q and ET; in Allen-Bradley a TON instruction with Preset, Accum and the DN bit; in Delta and Mitsubishi TMR or OUT T0 K50 (in 100ms units). The simulator here is a generic trainer with IEC / Siemens style addresses: the exact addresses and instructions differ from one brand to another, and the manual of your PLC is the reference.
