Mechanics

What you will learn

  • Convert units, tell mass from weight, and add forces as vectors
  • Work out speed, acceleration, braking distance and the path of anything thrown
  • Apply Newton’s three laws and calculate friction on the floor and on a ramp
  • Calculate work, energy and power in watts and horsepower, and efficiency
  • Understand torque, levers and gears, and the P = T·ω on every motor nameplate
  • Calculate pressure and a hydraulic jack’s force, and size a winch motor from scratch
Who it is for

Who this course is for

  • Anyone who wants the physics behind machines without heavy maths
  • Electrical and maintenance technicians who want to understand the load a motor drives
  • Diploma and engineering students as a quick, fun refresher
  • No experience needed
FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need strong maths?
No. Every equation comes with a worked example, and every calculation is multiply, divide and square root on a calculator.
Do the games work on a phone?
Yes. Every game runs in the browser; you move the sliders with your finger.
Is there a certificate?
Yes. Finish all the lessons and pass the final exam (80% or more), then get a course completion certificate from your account page.

Ready to brake your first car?

Lesson 1 is open to everyone, and the whole course opens when you buy it. Your progress is saved.
