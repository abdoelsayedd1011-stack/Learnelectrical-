Servo

What you'll learn

  • Wire the drive, motor, encoder and CN1 terminals
  • Test the motor with JOG from the keypad before any PLC
  • Run speed mode with a 0–10 V command and internal speeds
  • Send Pulse + Direction from a PLC and calculate the electronic gear
  • Use PR mode for preset positions and torque mode T
  • Tune gains with a heavy load and watch the motion profile
  • Secure a vertical axis with limit switches, E-stop and the brake
  • Understand alarms and clear them like on the real drive
Contents

Step 2: guided exercises

Each exercise has 4 steps: wiring, parameters, run, and try a fault. These are the Delta ASDA-B2 simulator exercises.

  1. JOG test from the keypad
    Make sure the motor and encoder are healthy before connecting a PLC
  2. Speed mode S with 0-10 V on V-REF
    Speed from an analog signal, with the servo enabled by SON
  3. Pulse mode PT: Pulse + Direction from a PLC
    Electronic gearing: how many pulses make one revolution
  4. PR mode: preset positions selected by inputs
    Positions stored in the drive, run by a signal
  5. Torque mode T: constant tension with T-REF and a speed limit
    Constant torque on a conveyor with a speed limit
  6. Vertical axis: limit switches, E-stop and brake
    How to keep the load from dropping when the servo switches off

FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need to know VFDs first?
Not required, but it helps a lot. Parameters, terminals and faults follow similar ideas.
Which brands are included?
Delta ASDA-B2, Mitsubishi MR-JE, Yaskawa Sigma-7 and Allen-Bradley Kinetix 300, each with its own keypad and display.
Do I need an account?
Yes, the servo simulators need an account, and your progress is saved to it.
Are the parameters identical to the manual?
Numbers are written for training and may differ from your version. Before any setting on a real machine, check the manufacturer's manual.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes, and the guided steps are designed to work on phones.
