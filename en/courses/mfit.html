Mechanical maintenance

What you will learn

  • Read a bolt’s size and grade and tighten it to the right torque in a star pattern
  • Choose the right key and coupling, align with shims and fix soft foot
  • Read a bearing number, fit it with a heater without damage, and choose the right O-ring and seal
  • Work out gear ratios, size a gearbox with a service factor and watch its oil
  • Tension a belt by deflection, measure chain stretch and grease the calculated amount
  • Tell a fault from its noise, heat, vibration or leak
Who it is for

Who this course is for

  • Electrical maintenance technicians who work next to fitters and want to understand their work
  • Beginners in factory mechanical maintenance
  • Technical diploma and engineering students as a practical start
  • Want the physics behind torque and gears? Mechanics basics complements this course
FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need experience first?
No. The course starts from the machine parts themselves and every number comes with an example.
Do the exercises work on a phone?
Yes. Every exercise is taps and sliders, designed for phones first.
Can I use these numbers on the job?
They are rounded for learning and give you the right feel. On the job always follow the maker’s catalogue.
Is there a certificate?
Yes, a certificate of completion when you finish every lesson and pass the final exam.

Ready to tighten your first flange?

Lesson 1 is open to everyone, and the whole course opens when you buy it. Your progress is saved.
