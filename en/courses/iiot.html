IIoT

What you'll learn

  • Set IP addresses and subnets, understand switches and VLANs, and why ping works while the protocol fails
  • Send a Modbus RTU request byte by byte with its CRC, and read a VFD and an energy meter on a correctly terminated RS-485 bus
  • Read and write Modbus TCP from a PLC, with the MBAP header and exception codes
  • Fix PROFIBUS addresses and GSD files, and name a new PROFINET device with DCP
  • Understand CAN arbitration and tell CANopen, DeviceNet, EtherNet/IP and EtherCAT apart
  • Know AS-i, IO-Link and HART and when each one fits
  • Connect to an OPC UA server with a certificate, and publish/subscribe on an MQTT broker with QoS, Retain and Last Will
  • Build a Node-RED gateway from the PLC to the cloud, and a dashboard with alarms that don't cry wolf, plus OEE
  • Read a vibration trend and predict a bearing failure before it happens
  • Troubleshoot any industrial network layer by layer with Wireshark, PRONETA and an ohmmeter
Simulator

Industrial network simulator: 11 exercises

Each exercise belongs to a lesson and counts toward your progress.

  1. IP addresses
    Four devices, one duplicate address and one on another subnet: fix them and ping
  2. Modbus RTU
    Termination, baud and parity; request and response in bytes with CRC and exception codes
  3. Modbus TCP
    Read the temperature and write a setpoint in a PLC, and see the MBAP header
  4. PROFIBUS DP
    Dial addresses, GSD files and termination at both ends
  5. PROFINET
    A replaced device lost its name: assign it with DCP and check PRONETA
  6. CAN bus
    Three nodes transmit at once: who wins? Measure the 60 Ω termination
  7. OPC UA
    Certificate and security policy, browse, read and subscribe
  8. MQTT
    Publish and subscribe with + and #, Retain and Last Will
  9. Node-RED gateway
    Wire Modbus → Function → MQTT and get correct JSON out
  10. Dashboard
    Alarm with limit, delay and hysteresis, OEE, and predict when vibration crosses the ISO limit
  11. Filling-line project
    Three hidden faults out of 8 in different layers: find them with the right tool and fix them

FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need hardware or software?
No. Every exercise runs in the simulator in your browser. You'll also learn which free tools to install for real networks (Wireshark, PRONETA, MQTT Explorer, UaExpert).
Which networks exactly?
TCP/IP, RS-485, Modbus RTU, Modbus TCP, PROFIBUS DP, PROFINET, CAN, CANopen, DeviceNet, EtherNet/IP, EtherCAT, AS-i, IO-Link, HART, OPC UA, MQTT and a Node-RED gateway.
Do I need an account?
The first lesson is free. The other lessons, the simulator and the certificate open when you buy the course, and your progress is saved to your account.
Is there a certificate?
Yes, a LearnElectricals completion certificate once you finish the lessons and exercises and pass the final exam with 80%.

Ready to connect the machine to the cloud?
