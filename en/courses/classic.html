FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need a background first?
It helps to know voltage, current, series and parallel. If not, take the electrical basics course first and come back.
How do the circuits work?
Each circuit is drawn ready. You press the buttons and it runs in front of you: contactors pick up, the motor turns, the overload heats and trips. In fault-finding mode the course hides a fault and you find it with the multimeter.
Is the course free?
The first two lessons are open to anyone with an account. The other lessons open when you buy the course.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes. The drawing and the panel scroll sideways on a small screen and the controls sit in the side column. A tablet or computer is more comfortable.

Ready to start classic control?

The first two lessons are open to try. Start now and continue at your own pace; your progress is saved.
