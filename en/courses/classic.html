FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need a background first?
It helps to know voltage, current, series and parallel. If not, take the electrical basics course first and come back.
How does the wiring work?
You drag a wire from terminal to terminal on a board like a real panel, then switch on. If something's wrong, the course shows where and why.
Is there a certificate?
Yes. When you finish every lesson and the final exam while signed in, you can get a Course Completion Certificate with a verification code and QR code. It confirms you completed the course; it is not an accredited qualification.
Is the course free?
The first lesson is open to everyone, no account needed. The other lessons need a free account you can create in seconds with your email.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes. The board fits small screens, but wiring is more comfortable on a tablet or computer.

Ready to start classic control?
