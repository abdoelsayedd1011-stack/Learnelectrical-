Step 2: guided exercises in every simulator

Each exercise has 4 steps: wiring, parameters, run, and try a fault. This is the PowerFlex 525 example; every simulator has exercises with the same idea for its device.

  1. Keypad + the drive's own potentiometer
    First run: Start from the keypad and speed from the built-in pot
  2. 2-wire: forward/reverse selector + external 0-10 V pot
    Run from terminals with an analog speed reference
  3. 3-wire: Start and Stop buttons + direction selector
    A momentary button and the drive latches itself
  4. Preset speeds from a 4-position selector
    Preset speeds on digital inputs
  5. 4-20 mA speed from a PLC + relay lamps and Hz meter
    A pump taking its speed from a PLC, with Ready, Fault and Run lamps
  6. Fast stop with a braking resistor (heavy load)
    Stop in a second and a half without an overvoltage fault

FAQ

Before you ask

Which drive should I start with?
If it's your first time, start with PowerFlex 525 and open “Start here”. Then open the drive you have at work.
Are the parameters identical to the manual?
Parameter numbers, terminals and faults are written for training and may differ from your version. Before any setting on a real device, check the manufacturer's manual for the same model.
Do I need an account?
The PowerFlex 525 simulator is open to everyone. The other simulators need an account, and your progress is saved to it.
Can I type the parameters myself?
Yes. In guided mode you enter every parameter from the keypad, and the simulator shows a ✓ as soon as the value is right.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes. The simulators work on phones, but the wiring diagram and parameter table are more comfortable on a bigger screen.
