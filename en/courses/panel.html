Panel design

What you'll learn

  • Tell main and sub-distribution boards, motor control centres, PFC panels and transfer panels apart, and know what IEC 61439 asks for
  • Calculate loads with demand and diversity factors, and work out the design current Ib for every feeder and the whole board
  • Pick the right MCB, MCCB or ACB, set Ir, Isd and Ii, and check the breaking capacities Icu and Ics
  • Size cables with the correction factors and the Ib ≤ In ≤ Iz rule
  • Work out voltage drop and short-circuit current at every point, and check the cable's thermal withstand
  • Design copper busbars and the earth and neutral bars, and work out the short-circuit forces on the supports
  • Make protective devices selective on time-current curves, and use cascading correctly
  • Choose the TN, TT or IT earthing system, the PE conductor size and the RCDs
  • Size a capacitor bank in kVAR, with its steps and its protection
  • Lay out the panel, choose the form of separation and the IP rating, work out the heat rise, and design MCC feeders to Type 2 coordination
Simulators

Panel designer's calculator and electrical panel designer: 11 exercises

A single-line diagram of a whole factory to calculate on, and a designer where you lay out the panel at real sizes. Each exercise belongs to a lesson and counts toward your progress.

  1. Load calculation
    A small factory: add up the installed power, apply the demand and diversity factors, and work out the design current Ib for every feeder and for the whole board
  2. Selecting breakers
    Pick an MCB, MCCB or ACB for every feeder, set Ir, Isd and Ii, and check that the breaking capacity Icu is above the short-circuit current at the board
  3. Cable sizing
    Apply the temperature, grouping and installation-method factors, and pick the smallest size that meets Ib ≤ In ≤ Iz
  4. Voltage drop and short circuit
    Work out the voltage drop to the last load and the short-circuit current at every board, and upsize the cable when the drop is over the limit or it fails k²S² ≥ I²t
  5. Copper busbars
    Size the busbar for current and temperature, and check the short-circuit forces, the support spacing and the earth and neutral bars
  6. Selectivity
    Move the breaker curves on the time-current chart until only the breaker closest to the fault trips
  7. Earthing and shock protection
    Choose the earthing system, the PE conductor size and the right RCD, and check that the disconnection time is within the limit
  8. Capacitor bank
    Work out the kVAR needed to correct the power factor, split it into steps, and choose the detuning reactors and the bank protection
  9. Panel layout and heat
    Choose the enclosure, place the components at their real sizes with the form of separation and the IP rating, and work out the temperature rise and ventilation
  10. MCC feeders
    Design DOL, star-delta, soft-starter and VFD feeders to Type 2 coordination, and pick the breaker, contactor and overload for each one
  11. Main distribution board project
    Design the factory's main distribution board from the calculation to the layout, and hand it over after the acceptance tests

FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need design software or catalogues?
No. Every exercise runs in the two simulators in your browser, and the tables you need are inside the lessons. When you design a real panel, you'll find the same steps and terms as in the standards and the catalogues.
Which standards does it cover?
IEC 60364 for installations, IEC 61439 for assemblies, IEC 60947 for breakers, IEC 60909 for short-circuit currents and IEC TR 60890 for panel heat rise, together with the Egyptian code for electrical installations.
Do I need an account?
The first lesson is free. The other lessons, both simulators and the certificate open when you buy the course, and your progress is saved to your account.
Is there a certificate?
Yes, a LearnElectricals completion certificate once you finish the lessons and exercises and pass the final exam with 80%.

Ready to design your first panel?

The first lesson is free; the whole course with both simulators opens when you buy it. Start now and continue at your own pace; your progress is saved.
