What you'll learn

  • Read loops and tags: TT, PT and FT, PV, SP and range
  • Convert 4–20 mA to a real value, and know why it starts at 4
  • Wire 2-, 3- and 4-wire transmitters correctly
  • Tell PNP from NPN and wire them to a PLC input card

  • Choose and wire thermocouples and PT100, and spot wiring errors

FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need a background first?
You should understand voltage, current and resistance and be able to use a multimeter. If not, start with the electrical basics course.
Is it about a specific brand?
No. The course explains the ideas that are the same across brands, with examples from devices common on the market.
Is there a certificate?
Yes. When you finish every lesson and the final exam while signed in, you can get a Course Completion Certificate with a verification code and QR code. It confirms you completed the course; it is not an accredited qualification.
Is the course free?
The first lesson is free with a free account. The other lessons need a free account you can create in seconds with your email.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes. Every bench and experiment works on phones, tablets and computers.
