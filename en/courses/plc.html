In order. Every lesson has a short explanation, hands-on experiments and questions.

FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need a background first?
You'll get more out of it if you understand contactor control circuits: seal-in, interlocks and star/delta. If not, start with the classic control course.
Is it for a specific PLC brand?
The simulator is a general trainer with IEC/Siemens-style addresses like I0.0 and Q0.0. The logic is the same on every PLC; only the address format and instruction names differ. There are also Siemens-, Delta- and Allen-Bradley-style simulators, each with 8 exercises from real plants.
Do I need to install software?
No. The Ladder simulator runs in the browser on phones and computers. It doesn't replace vendor software like TIA Portal or Studio 5000.
Is there a certificate?
Yes. When you finish every lesson and the final exam while signed in, you can get a Course Completion Certificate with a verification code and QR code. It confirms you completed the course; it is not an accredited qualification.
Is the course free?
The first two lessons and lesson 5 are open to everyone, no account needed. The other lessons need a free account you can create in seconds with your email.
