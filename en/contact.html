To get a faster reply

  • Reporting a mistake: name the simulator and the parameter number, the correct value and its source (manual page number).
  • Requesting a device: give the exact brand and model, and what you use it for.
  • Training centre: tell us how many students you have and which devices you train on.

We reply in English or Arabic. The site has no form that sends data to a server; your message goes straight from your own WhatsApp or email.
