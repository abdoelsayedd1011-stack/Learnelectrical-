Power and energy

How much does this appliance "eat"? And why does the water heater push the bill up while a lamp doesn’t? We will understand the watt and the kilowatt-hour, work out the bill for a whole home, and see why a wire heats up and a fuse blows.

1Power = how fast energy is used

⚙️Power in watts W: 1 watt = 1 joule per second
P = V × I
Know current and resistanceP = I² × R
Know voltage and resistanceP = V² ÷ R

Voltage drops to half ⇒ power drops to a quarter. A 2000 W heater on 110 V = only 500 W.

4Energy = power × time

kWh = (W ÷ 1000) × h
🧾The meter counts kWh. 1 kWh = 1000 W for one hour.

A home in one day ≈ 15 kWh

  • 💡 6 LED bulbs
    0.36 kWh
  • 🧊 Fridge
    1.5 kWh
  • 🚿 Heater 1 h
    2 kWh
  • ❄️ AC 8 h
    11.2 kWh

The AC alone is about two thirds of the bill.

6Efficiency

η = Pout ÷ Pin × 100%
  • 💡 Filament bulb
    5%

    35%
  • 🌀 Small motor

The rest is lost as heat. A motor giving 1.5 kW at 85% draws 1.76 kW.
