Wiring a lamp and a socket

Time for hands-on work. You will wire 4 real home circuits wire by wire, switch the power on and flip the switches, and the simulator tells you whether the wiring is right or wrong and why, and also when it works but is not safe.

1Before you wire

  • The switch always goes on the line (brown). The line goes into the switch and out of it to the lamp, and the neutral (blue) goes straight to the lamp.
  • In the lamp holder: the switched line goes to the centre contact (L) and the neutral to the screw thread (N), so the part your finger might touch is the neutral.
  • The socket: brown to L, blue to N, green/yellow to earth. Never leave the earth unconnected.
  • Any switch or socket is wired with the breaker OFF. In this simulator too: you can’t touch a wire while the power is on.
Why is the switch on the neutral wrong if the lamp works? When you switch it off, the lamp really does go out, but the line is still connected to the lamp holder. If you hold a tester on the holder it will light! And whoever changes the bulb while it is "off" could get a shock. The simulator will catch this mistake.
