Common household faults

The customer calls and describes the problem, and you go with your tester and multimeter. In each game there is a hidden fault: measure, think, and choose the right repair. And at the end comes the final exam for the course.

1How to think

  1. Listen to the complaint and look for yourself: has the breaker tripped? Is there a burning smell? Has anything melted?
  2. Follow the electricity from the panel to the appliance, point by point: where is the line present, and where does it disappear?
  3. The tester shows you quickly where the line is, and the multimeter on V~ tells you the voltage between two points.
  4. Does the breaker trip? Switch the power off and measure ohms: a short shows as a reading close to zero.
The open-neutral trick: if the neutral is broken after a lamp and the switch is ON, the line passes through the filament and reaches the neutral wire. The tester lights on both terminals while the lamp is off! And the multimeter between the two terminals reads almost zero.

2The game: find the fault

Choose the complaint. There is a random hidden fault, and it changes every time you choose "Another fault". You need to solve each complaint correctly at least once.
