Conductivity: conductors and insulators

Why is a wire copper on the inside and plastic on the outside? And why is water dangerous with electricity? You will try lots of materials on a tester, and work out wire resistance and voltage drop yourself.

3Water and your body

✋Dry handhigh resistance
vs

6Heat raises resistance

🧊100 W bulb, cold38 Ω
vs
🔥Same bulb, lit480 Ω

Copper rises 0.4% per degree. That’s why a multimeter reads a bulb low.

7Copper or aluminium?

🏠Copper + PVChome and factory cables
flexible, great conductor
vs
🗼Aluminiumoverhead lines

Never join copper to aluminium directly: it corrodes, heats and burns. Use a bi-metal joint.
