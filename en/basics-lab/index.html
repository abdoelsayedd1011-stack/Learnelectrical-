LearnElectricals · Course 1

Electrical Basics for Beginners

If you have never held a tester screwdriver, start here. You will understand how electricity works: voltage and current, conductors and insulators, series and parallel, and power and the electricity bill. Then you pick up the multimeter and use it to measure the socket, the water heater and the motor, wire a lamp, a socket and a two-way switch yourself, and track down home faults. Every lesson has a simple explanation, interactive experiments, practical examples and questions.

How to study here

  1. Read the explanation: one idea per part, in plain words
  2. Try it yourself: move, tap, measure and wire
  3. Look at the examples from the home and the workshop
  4. Answer the questions so the lesson is ticked off
