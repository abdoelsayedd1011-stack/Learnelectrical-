What is electricity?

Before you pick up a screwdriver or a multimeter, you only need to understand 4 words: voltage, current, resistance and power. We will explain them with water in pipes, and then work out an electricity bill ourselves.

3Power: how much does it use?

P = V × I ⇒ I = P ÷ 220

Current at 220 V

  • 👕 Iron
    1200 W · 5.5 A
  • ❄️ 1.5 hp AC
    1400 W · 6.4 A
  • 🚿 Water heater
    2000 W · 9.1 A

One heater = the current of 180 LED bulbs. That’s why it needs a thicker wire and its own breaker.
