What is electricity?

First things first: what is electricity? We look inside the wire, see what current means, and where electricity comes from.

💡LightLamps
🔥HeatHeaters and irons
🌀MotionFans and motors
📱Sound and picturePhones and TVs

2Inside the wire: atoms

+Nucleusin the middle
vs
−Electronsorbit around it

Copper has free electrons. A battery pushes them one way = current.

3Current = moving electrons

〰️When the free electrons all move in one direction, that is called electric current.

Experiment: electrons inside the wire

🔓Switch openElectrons jiggle in place
Lamp off
vs
🔒Switch closedElectrons move one way
Lamp on

Like water in a pipe: the pipe is already full, and when you open the tap all the water moves at once.

4Where does electricity come from?

🏭Power stationProduces it
🗼Towers and cablesCarry it to you
🏠Meter and socketElectricity at home
🔋BatteryA small source you carry

5Be careful

Electricity has no colour and no smell: only a tester tells you it is there. Never touch a bare wire.
