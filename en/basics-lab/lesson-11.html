Ohm’s law, power and choosing the cable and breaker

From the power written on an appliance you will work out the current, and from that choose the right breaker and a cable size that can carry it without heating up.

3Example: 3000 W heater

  1. Current 13.6A
  2. 16A breaker

🛡️Size the wire for the breaker, not the appliance.

A 25 A breaker on 1.5 mm² wire = the wire melts before the breaker trips.

Long run or continuous load? Go one step up: 4 mm² and a 20 A breaker.

4The usual table

  • 1.5 mm² · 10 Alighting
  • 2.5 mm² · 16 Asockets, heater up to 3000 W
  • 4 mm² · 20–25 Aheater, AC, oven
  • 6 mm² · 32 Aelectric cooker, feeder
  • 10 mm² · 40 Amain flat supply

Breakers: 6 · 10 · 16 · 20 · 25 · 32 · 40 A. Curve C for motors and AC units.

This is a rough guide. Follow your local code and an engineer for big loads.
