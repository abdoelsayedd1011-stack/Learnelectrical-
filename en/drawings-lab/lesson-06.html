Reading drawings · Lesson 6 of 7

Panel layout and terminal strips

When you open the panel door you will find hundreds of wires. Two drawings let you find your way as if you already knew the panel: the panel layout (where each device is) and the terminal diagram (where each wire comes from and goes to). At the end you will trace signals from the field to the PLC yourself.
