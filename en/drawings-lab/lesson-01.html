Reading drawings · Lesson 1 of 7

Types of electrical drawings

The drawing set for a machine or a panel can run to 80 pages. You will not read them all: each type of drawing answers one question. In this lesson you will learn the main types, tell them apart at a glance, and know which one to open when you are standing in front of a fault.
