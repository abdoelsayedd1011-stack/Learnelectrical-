Symbols: IEC and ANSI/NEMA

Symbols are the alphabet of drawings. There are two schools: IEC (European, and most of our region) and ANSI/NEMA (American, which you will meet on American machines). In this lesson you will see both side by side, review them with cards, and practise until you can recognise any symbol at a glance.
