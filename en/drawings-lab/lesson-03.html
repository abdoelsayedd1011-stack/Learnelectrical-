Reading drawings · Lesson 3 of 7

Device tags, terminal numbers and cross-references

Symbols tell you what kind of device it is, but the numbers are what lead you to it in the panel. In this lesson you will learn why each device is named the way it is, why its terminals are numbered the way they are, and how to jump from a contact to its coil on another page with the cross-reference.
