Reading Electrical Drawings

Drawings are the electrician's language: if you can read them, you can find a fault in any panel, even one you have never seen before. In this course you will learn the types of drawings, read IEC and NEMA symbols, understand terminal numbers and cross-references, and read the control circuit, the single line and the panel and terminal drawings. At the end you will trace faults from the drawing with a multimeter.

Before you start: this course assumes you understand voltage and current, can use a multimeter, and know how contactors and relays work. If not yet, start with the Electrical Basics course → and the Classic Control course →, then come back here.

How to study here

  1. Read the explanation: one idea per part, in plain words
  2. Open the drawing: pinch or press + to zoom, and tap any device or reference
  3. Run the circuit: press the buttons and see where the current flows
  4. Answer the exercises and questions to complete the lesson
