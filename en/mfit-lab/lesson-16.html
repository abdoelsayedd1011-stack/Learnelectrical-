The machine is complaining: understand it

Every mechanical fault has a sign. Listen, feel and measure before you take anything apart.

‹ LessonsAll courses →

1Your tools

🩺Stethoscope (or screwdriver)on the bearing housing
🌡️IR thermometercompare with past days
📳Vibration penvertical, horizontal, axial
📒The logbookone reading is not enough; the trend matters

2Sign → cause

📳Radial vibration at running speedunbalance
↔️High axial vibrationmisalignment
🔩Knocking, rattlinglooseness: bolts or base
🔊Rumble or grindinga failing bearing
😱Squeal at starta slipping belt
💧Leaka seal or blocked breather

3Temperature

  • 🟢 Normal
    < 70 °C
  • 🟡 Watch
    70 – 85 °C
  • 🔴 Stop and inspect
    > 85 °C

Rough values for a bearing housing. What matters most: a sudden rise of 10–15 °C over normal means something is wrong.

4The inspection plan

Listen→Feel the heat→Measure→Compare with the log→Decide

Inspect running machines without removing guards. Any dismantling = isolate and lock off.
