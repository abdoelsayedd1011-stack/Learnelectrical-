Two shafts, one line

Most bearing, coupling and seal failures start with poor alignment. Good alignment extends the life of the whole machine.

↕️Each is measured in two planes: vertical (fixed with shims) and horizontal (fixed by moving the motor sideways).

2Measuring tools

  • 📏 Straightedge + feeler
    ≈ 0.1 mm
  • ⏱️ Dial indicator
    0.01 mm
  • 🔴 Laser
    0.001 mm

A straightedge is only for rough setting. A 1500 rpm motor needs a dial or laser.

3First: soft foot

🦶One motor foot does not sit flat. Tightening it twists the whole frame.
Dial on the foot→Loosen its bolt→Moves > 0.05?→Shim it

Shims: clean, stainless, pre-cut. No more than 3–4 under one foot.

4Acceptable limits (around 1500 rpm)

Offset≤ 0.05mm
Angle≤ 0.05mm / 100 mm
🧠Order: fix the angle first with the difference between front and rear feet, then raise both equally for the offset.

The faster the machine, the tighter the limits. Torque the feet and measure again at the end.
