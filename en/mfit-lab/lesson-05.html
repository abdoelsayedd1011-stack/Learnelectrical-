The coupling: the joint that forgives

Motor and load shafts are never perfectly in line. A flexible coupling absorbs the difference.

3How to choose

Torque→Speed→Shock→Misalignment
📈Size a coupling by load torque × service factor (1.5–2 for shock loads).

Spider worn out? Fit the same hardness and colour, and replace it before the metal jaws touch.
