A drop of oil is a warning

An O-ring seals between still parts; an oil seal seals on a turning shaft. Both are cheap, but getting them wrong is expensive.

‹ LessonsAll courses →

1The O-ring: just two sizes

⭕IDinside diameter
⚫CScross-section
📏Written ID × CS, e.g. 50 × 3. It must be squeezed about 15–25% in its groove to seal.

Never measure an old O-ring; it has stretched or flattened. Measure the groove.

2Material by fluid and temperature

⚫NBR (Buna)oil, diesel · −30 → 100 °C · most common
🟤Viton FKMup to 200 °C and chemicals
🟣EPDMwater, steam · never with oil
⚪Siliconefood · weak when moving

An EPDM O-ring in hydraulic oil swells and fails within days.

3The oil seal: lip towards the oil

Shaft35mm
Housing52mm
Width7mm
🎯The lip and spring face the oil (what stays in). The small dust lip faces out.

35 × 52 × 7 = shaft × housing × width. If the old lip wore a groove, set the new seal slightly deeper or fit a sleeve.

4Fitting without damage

🧴Grease the lipwith a compatible grease
🩹Cover threads and keywayswith a sleeve or tape
🔨Drive with a flat toolso it goes in square
🔄No twistinga twisted O-ring leaks

Seal leaking for no clear reason? Check the breather: a blocked one pushes oil past the seal.
