Inside the machine

Every factory machine is a motor + parts that carry the motion + a load. Learn them once and you will see them everywhere.

‹ LessonsAll courses →

1From motor to load

🔁The motor turns fast and weak; the load needs slow and strong. Everything in between converts one into the other.
⚡ Motor→🔗 Coupling→⚙️ Gearbox→⛓️ Chain→🛞 Load

Under every turning shaft there are bearings, and it is all held to the frame with bolts.

2Each part and its job

🔗Couplingjoins two shafts end to end
⚙️Gearboxcuts speed, multiplies torque
➰Belt and pulleycarries motion to a far shaft, quietly
⛓️Chainlike a belt that cannot slip
🛞Bearingcarries the shaft, lets it turn smoothly
🔑Keystops a gear slipping on its shaft

3Speed falls… torque rises

1450 rpmAt the motorfast
torque ≈ 10 N·m
vs
48 rpmAt the drumslow
torque ≈ 275 N·m

Torque along the conveyor (gearbox 1:20, chain 1:1.5)

  • ⚡ Motor
    10 N·m
  • ⚙️ Gearbox
    190 N·m
  • ⛓️ Drum
    275 N·m
💡Cut the speed 20 times? Torque rises about 20 times, minus a little lost as heat.

That is why the slowest shaft is the one that shears keys and bolts: it carries the most torque.

44 things that ruin any machine

🔩Loose jointsa loose bolt or a worn key
📐Misalignmenttwo shafts not in line
🛢️Wrong lubricationtoo little, too much or wrong type
↔️Wrong tensiona belt or chain too slack or too tight

Each one has its own lesson in this course. A tired machine tells you with noise, heat or vibration.

Before your hand touches any part: isolate the power and lock it off (LOTO).
