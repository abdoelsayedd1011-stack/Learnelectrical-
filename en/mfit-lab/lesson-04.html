How does a pulley hold on a shaft?

Two things must be stopped: the pulley turning on the shaft, and it sliding along it.

🔄Stop it turninga key
or a taper-lock bush

↔️Stop it slidingset screw on the key
circlip or shoulder

2Key size from shaft diameter

Ø22 – 308 × 7width × height
Ø30 – 3810 × 8mm
Ø38 – 4412 × 8mm
Ø44 – 5014 × 9mm
Ø50 – 5816 × 10mm
🔑A key must be a tight fit on its sides with a small gap on top.

A loose key wears the keyway and ruins the shaft. Replace it early.

3The taper-lock bush

🧩A tapered bush that wedges into the pulley as you tighten its screws, gripping the shaft all round.
Clean→Fit→Tighten alternately→Tap and re-tighten

To remove: move a screw into the threaded jacking hole to push the bush out. Never hammer the pulley.

4Stopping it sliding

🔩Set screwon top of the key, with locker
⭕External circlipin a groove on the shaft
◎Internal circlipin a groove in the housing
🧱Shaft shoulderthe part sits against it

Fit and remove circlips only with circlip pliers.
