Tighten by number, not by feel

A bolt works like a spring: it must stretch just the right amount to clamp. Torque sets that amount.

🎯Right torque = the bolt stretched within its springy range.

2Torque table (steel 8.8, dry thread)

  • M8
    25 N·m
  • M10
    49 N·m
  • M12
    85 N·m
  • M16
    210 N·m
  • M20
    410 N·m

10.9 ≈ torque × 1.4. A lubricated thread needs about 20% less, or you will over-tighten.

The maker’s table always wins, especially on flanges and gaskets.

3The torque wrench

🔧Set the valueon the handle
🐢Pull slowlyfrom the grip centre
🔊First click = stopnever pull on
📦Back to minimumbefore storing

Never use a torque wrench to undo bolts; it ruins the calibration.

4Star pattern, in stages

⭐Never go round the circle. Tighten a bolt, then the one opposite.
30%→70%→100%→check pass

8 bolts: 1-5-3-7-2-6-4-8. Same order every stage, then a final round at full torque.
