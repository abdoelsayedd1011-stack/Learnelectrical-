Chains stretch: measure them

A chain does not stretch like a belt: its pins and bushes wear so the pitch grows. At 3% growth it is finished.

Measure over at least 10 pitches with light tension. Sprocket teeth hooked? Replace the sprockets with the chain.

4Lubrication

💧Oil (not grease) into the gaps between the plates on the slack side, so it reaches the pin and bush.

Never oil or measure a running chain; it drags a hand into the sprocket in a second.
