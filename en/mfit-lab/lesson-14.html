Grease: more is not better

Too little grease leaves a bearing dry; too much overheats it. The right amount is calculated from its size.

stays put · bearings

🛢️Oilcirculates and cools

2The numbers on the tub

🧈NLGI 2the usual for bearings
🧱NLGI 3stiffer · heat or vertical shafts
💧NLGI 0 / 00soft · gears and central systems
🛢️ISO VG 68 / 220oil viscosity: hydraulics / gears

Never mix two greases (e.g. lithium with polyurea); they can soften or harden.

3The right amount

G (g) = 0.005 × D × B
⚖️D is outside diameter and B width in mm. A 6310: 0.005 × 110 × 27 ≈ 15 g.

Know your gun: weigh 10 strokes and divide. Typical is 1–1.5 g per stroke.

4How to grease correctly

Wipe the nipple→Open the drain plug→The calculated amount→Run 30 minutes→Close the drain

Temperature rises a little after greasing and settles within an hour. If it stays high, you overdid it.
