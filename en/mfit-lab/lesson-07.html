The micrometer: 0.01 mm accuracy

Before fitting a bearing or bush you must measure the shaft. Reading = sleeve scale + thimble scale.

👀Read the last mark visible before the thimble edge, above and below.

3The circular scale (on the thimble)

🔄The thimble has 50 divisions. One full turn moves 0.5 mm, so each division = 0.01 mm.

Read the division sitting on the sleeve line and multiply by 0.01.

4Reading in 3 steps

Whole mm12above the line
Half mm+ 0.5lower mark visible
Thimble+ 0.3737 × 0.01
12 + 0.5 + 0.37 = 12.87 mm

Before measuring: clean both faces and close with the ratchet. It must read zero; if not, zero it with its spanner.

Never force the thimble. Use the ratchet so the pressure, and the reading, stay consistent.
