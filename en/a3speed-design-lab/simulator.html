Design Correction simulator for the A3/Speed

From reading the printed register mark to correcting the crease position on the package. All values here are for learning only.

Try a scenario:
Press Start. The machine feeds the web one package at a time, the photocells read the printed mark, and the TMCC adjusts the folding flaps so the mark arrives at the right moment relative to the jaws.

Machine, side view

Offset control0
Buttons are in mm: 0.1 mm = 10 offset units. Tap for one step, or hold and the value keeps changing.

Zoomed angle window

Angles here are at true scale, without the drawing's exaggeration: the green band is the accepted ±1.5 mm range around the production angle, and the blue dots are the signal arrival angle of the last few packages. When you change the offset the green band moves, then the dots follow it.

The closed jaw travels down with the tube: that is the main feed. The folding flaps ride on it and add a small extra pull that depends on the stroke. The photocell lights up blue at the moment the black mark passes it, and at that same moment the encoder dial records the angle. The deviation is exaggerated in the drawing and on the dial so you can see it; the written numbers are the real ones. The dashed orange line on the web is the printed crease, and the black square is the mark the photocell reads.

What the photocell just read:
Pattern stored in the TMCC:

Black = 1, white = 0, white to black = 1, black to white = 0. Seeing a colour does not mean the code was read: all 26 bits must be read in the right order before the design signal is generated.
